# Millennium Dawn: United Kingdom Rework

Britain enters the new millennium smaller than it has ever been. Scotland and Wales have their own parliaments, Ireland is long gone, and the old realms across the seas only share a Queen. This submod for [Millennium Dawn](https://github.com/MillenniumDawn/Millennium-Dawn) asks one question. Can the United Kingdom be put back together, and how far are you willing to go to do it?

## The Union Question

At the heart of the rework is a brand new focus branch called The Union Question. It gives you two very different ways to rebuild British power.

**A Family of Nations.** Win people over. Royal tours, development grants, trade deals and a lot of patience slowly bring nations closer until they choose to join you.

**The Crown Imperial.** Stop asking. Suspend the devolved assemblies, send ultimatums, blockade Dublin and take back what was lost. It works, but the rest of the world is watching.

Either way, the road runs through the Home Nations, Ireland, the Crown realms like Canada, Australia, New Zealand and the Caribbean, the old overseas territories from Gibraltar to Hong Kong, and on the darkest path even India, Suez and the Thirteen Colonies.

## Consent, strain and consequences

Every country on the list climbs a ladder from Aligned to Associated to Constituent and finally Integrated. Each step needs their consent, and a refusal can shut the door for years.

Push too hard at home and Union Strain starts to bite. Push too hard abroad and Imperial Tension brings EU sanctions, a cold shoulder from Washington, trouble in NATO and eventually foreign guns in the hands of rebels. Meanwhile Commonwealth Cohesion decides whether your old partners stand with you or walk away.

The countries you target are not sitting ducks either. They can run sovereignty campaigns, demand home rule, find a foreign protector or rise up against you.

## Also in this update

- A new Commonwealth of Nations category with the Commonwealth Games, summits, a trade area and a defence pact
- Reworked Devolution GUI and moved it into the Devolution Decision Category. Also the map updates, based on the current situation of the dependencies
- A redesigned Union Question panel that shows every nation and how close it is to joining
- Queen Elizabeth II as head of state for every Commonwealth realm at the 2000 start
- Around 200 new events, from an Irexit referendum to a Scottish parliament in exile
- AI that knows how to play both paths

The old "A More United Kingdom" and "Fractured Kingdom" branches from Millennium Dawn have been replaced by The Union Question.

## Installation

1. Subscribe to Millennium Dawn and this mod.
2. Enable both in the launcher, with this mod loaded after Millennium Dawn.

Current version 0.1.0 is built for Millennium Dawn 2.0.1 and Hearts of Iron IV 1.19. It changes the United Kingdom files, so it will clash with other mods that edit the UK.
