## What you get

- **The Union Question focus branch.** 56 new focuses split over two mutually exclusive paths. It replaces Millennium Dawn's "A More United Kingdom" and "Fractured Kingdom" branches.
- **A union ladder for dozens of countries.** Each target climbs from Aligned to Associated to Constituent and finally Integrated. Targets include the Home Nations, Ireland, the Crown realms, the overseas territories (Chagos, the Falklands, Gibraltar, Hong Kong, Malta and Cyprus) and six wider Commonwealth regions from West Africa to the Pacific. On the Crown Imperial path, nations can also end up as Crown Colonies.
- **Three pressures to manage.** Union Strain drains political power and stability at home. Imperial Tension brings opinion penalties, EU trade penalties, UN sanctions, NATO strain and foreign-armed rebels. Commonwealth Cohesion decides whether your old partners back you or censure you.
- **Targets fight back.** Target countries get their own decisions to run sovereignty campaigns, demand home rule or seek a foreign guarantor.
- **A Commonwealth of Nations decision category.** Host the Commonwealth Games, face a heads of government summit every two years, and build a trade area, a development fund and a defence pact that forms its own faction.
- **An imperial end game.** On the Crown Imperial path only, reclaim the Thirteen Colonies, restore the British Raj and return to Suez.
- **A new Union Question panel.** One screen shows every target nation, its stage and how close it is to agreeing.
- **A reworked Devolution panel.** Millennium Dawn's devolution panel now lives in the Devolved Matters decision category and adds Ireland. The maps of Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland and Ireland redraw as their status changes.
- **Over 170 new events.** They include an Irexit referendum, a Welsh Senedd in exile, Quebec sovereignty, an Australian republic referendum, the Chagos case at The Hague, Gibraltar and Hong Kong.
- **The British Crown as head of state.** Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Papua New Guinea, the Solomon Islands, Tuvalu and nine Caribbean realms start the game with a head-of-state spirit for the Crown.
- **AI that plays both paths.**

## The Union Question

At the heart of the rework is a brand new focus branch called The Union Question. It gives you two very different ways to rebuild British power.

**A Family of Nations.** Win people over. Royal tours, development grants, trade deals and a lot of patience slowly bring nations closer until they choose to join you.

**The Crown Imperial.** Stop asking. Suspend the devolved assemblies, send ultimatums, blockade Ireland and take back what was lost. It works, but the rest of the world is watching.

Either way, the road runs through the Home Nations, Ireland, the Crown realms like Canada, Australia, New Zealand and the Caribbean, and the old overseas territories from Gibraltar to Hong Kong. On the darkest path it reaches even India, Suez and the Thirteen Colonies.

## Consent, strain and consequences

Every country on the list climbs a ladder from Aligned to Associated to Constituent and finally Integrated. On the Family path each step needs their consent, and a refusal can shut the door for years. The Crown path skips the asking. It relies on ultimatums, occupation and Crown Colony status instead.

Push too hard at home and Union Strain starts to bite. Push too hard abroad and Imperial Tension brings EU sanctions, a cold shoulder from Washington, trouble in NATO and eventually foreign guns in the hands of rebels. Meanwhile Commonwealth Cohesion decides whether your old partners stand with you or walk away.

The countries you target are not sitting ducks either. They can run sovereignty campaigns, demand home rule, find a foreign protector or rise up against you.

## What it replaces

- The "A More United Kingdom" and "Fractured Kingdom" focus branches, and their decision categories, are replaced by The Union Question.
- The mod changes the United Kingdom files, so it will clash with other mods that edit the UK.

## Installation

1. Subscribe to Millennium Dawn and this mod.
2. Enable both in the launcher, with this mod loaded after Millennium Dawn.
3. Start a new game after installing or updating.

Current version 0.1.0 is built for Millennium Dawn 2.0.1 and Hearts of Iron IV 1.19.
